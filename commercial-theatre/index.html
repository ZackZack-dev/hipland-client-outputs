02 / Services
System Design & Consulting

Bespoke systems for new productions, long-running shows, and redevelopments. Coverage prediction, system architecture, rigging design, networking, gain structure, RF planning, and FOH workflow.

Show Operation & Crew

Theatre-trained engineers with decades of experience. FOH operators, RF technicians, A2/A3 support crew, bump-in/bump-out technical teams, touring support and swings.
