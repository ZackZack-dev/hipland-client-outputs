About

Sound
engineering
since 1979

One of Australia's most recognisable and historic sound companies. Built on technical excellence, commitment to quality, and genuine care for every production.

01 / Story

System Sound was founded in 1979 by owner and managing director John Scandrett. The company was built on technical excellence, commitment to quality and technical innovation. Our history spans back to the prolific JC Williamson Theatre Company, where John Scandrett worked as one of Australia's first theatre sound designers.

As a recently graduated physicist with a passion for electronics and as a violinist in a chamber orchestra, John was uniquely skilled to meet the requirements of theatrical sound reinforcement. Over the years, the team he built set System Sound to become the go-to name for high quality theatrical products and all events requiring world class sound engineering.

By the 2000s, we had designed and provided sound for hundreds of productions and events, some of which — like The Phantom of the Opera and the Hong Kong Handover of 1997 — defined an era of exponential expansion in technical innovation and the development of live entertainment itself.

02 / Approach

How
we work

Our uncompromising approach to service is what sets us apart. Meticulous planning and seamless execution minimise downtime, enabling fast, well-ordered turnarounds on every job, big or small. We understand the daily realities of theatre and the demands of long-term performance.

We empower local designers and production teams with best-in-class tools and technology — including Meyer Sound loudspeakers and control systems — to approach any project with the same enthusiasm and deliver a top-tier, seamless result. From school musicals to the biggest theatre productions in the world — we service them all with equal energy.

04 / Orchestral & Symphonic

System Sound specialises in orchestral sound reinforcement and works with the Hong Kong Philharmonic, KBS Orchestra (Seoul), The Singapore Symphony, Orchestra Victoria, the Australian Pops Orchestra, The Sydney Opera House, The Arts Centre Melbourne.

Large scale projects include the Hong Kong and Macau Handovers, Melbourne's Millennium New Year's Eve Celebrations, and stadium concerts with Dame Kiri Te Kanawa, José Carreras, Plácido Domingo, Andrea Bocelli, and Luciano Pavarotti.
