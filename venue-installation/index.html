Custom audio system design, installation, and commissioning for theatres, venues, churches, schools, hospitality, and public spaces.

01 / What We Offer

With 45+ years of experience and direct relationships with the world's best equipment manufacturers — including being a direct Meyer Sound distributor — System Sound offers end-to-end venue installation services that deliver premium, long-term audio solutions. We don't just install speakers. We design systems that work for your space, your users, and your budget.

  • Venue assessment and acoustic analysis
  • System design and coverage prediction
  • Equipment specification and supply — direct pricing
  • Professional installation, rigging, cabling
  • Commissioning and tuning
  • User training and documentation
  • Ongoing support and maintenance
02 / Process
01 Listen
