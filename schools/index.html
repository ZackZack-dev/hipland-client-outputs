Professional sound system installation and live audio support for schools across Victoria. Theatre-grade audio trusted by Australia's top performing arts venues — brought into your school.

01 / Why System Sound
Theatre Expertise

Over 45 years of experience in musical theatre sound design, working on Australia's most iconic productions. We bring that expertise directly into your school hall or performing arts centre.

Learning Pathways

We work alongside drama teachers, AV coordinators, and music departments to offer student training, work experience, and career mentorship. Several of our student collaborators have gone on to professional sound engineering careers.

Tailored Systems

From school halls to purpose-built theatres. Meyer Sound systems, Sennheiser wireless kits, full-stage monitoring. Long-term value, ease of use, broadcast-quality performance.
